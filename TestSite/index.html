Get answers

What are the plan tiers?

Bronze plans have the lowest premiums and highest out-of-pocket costs. Platinum plans have the highest premiums and lowest out-of-pocket costs. Silver and Gold are in between.

What's a premium vs. a deductible?

A premium is what you pay each month for coverage. A deductible is what you pay for care before your plan starts paying.

How is my savings estimate calculated?

On this demo site, savings are a simplified illustration based on household size and income. They are not real eligibility results.

Can I change plans later?

You can change plans during Open Enrollment, or after a qualifying life event.
